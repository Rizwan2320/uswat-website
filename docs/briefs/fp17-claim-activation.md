# Brief FP-17: claim by invitation and activation by the department head (FP-01, AUTH-01, PJ1, section 5.6; competition tier)

Branch `fp/claim-activation`, stacked on `fp/review-queue` (PR #115): migration numbering continues from 0011. Merge #115 first. Date 2026-11-21. All people and departments are Sample data (owner decision 2026-11-20); no email is sent because there is no mailer, so the invitation link is printed by an operator command.

## Objective
Today the only way to get a portal account is a developer script. The spec says accounts come only from an imported People record, through a single-use link valid 72 hours, bound to that record, and stay inactive until the department head confirms "this is our faculty member". PJ1: invitation, enrol a passkey, department head activates, sign in.

## Design
- **Invitation.** `pnpm identity:invite <record> <email> <name> <department>` (operator only) creates one row in `identity.invitation` and prints the claim link once. Only a hash of the token is stored. 72 hours. One open invitation per record; inviting again revokes the previous one. Sample record references, not real People ids.
- **Claim.** `/portal/claim/[token]` shows the name and the invited address and asks for a password of at least 15 characters (the library enforces it). The token is read without being used; the account is created through the library; then, in one transaction, the token is consumed (only if still unused and unexpired), the account gets status `pending-activation`, its department and a claim record. If the token was used in between, the new account is removed and the person is told the link no longer works. The page for a bad, used or expired token looks the same for all three and reveals nothing about the record.
- **Account status.** `identity.account_status`: `pending-activation`, `active`, `suspended`, `left`. An account with no row is active (the existing sample accounts); only the claim flow creates rows. The status is read in the one place both the API and the pages read the session (`readSessionState`). Pending: may enrol a factor and sign out, and sees `/portal/pending` ("waiting for your department head"); every other portal page and endpoint is refused. Suspended or left: the session is ended and the caller is treated as signed out. If the status cannot be read, the portal is closed to that caller.
- **Activation.** A new role, department head (`identity.head_role`, set by `pnpm identity:roles grant-head`, needs a department), lists pending accounts in their own department that have enrolled a second factor, and can Activate or Decline (decline sets `suspended` and ends their sessions). Needs a fresh second factor. One transaction: head role locked, account row locked, still pending, same department, not self; log row in `identity.account_review` (append-only, same rules as the profile log). A head's own account cannot be activated by themselves.
- **Not self-registration.** `/sign-up/email` stays forbidden on the route. The claim action calls the library from the server only after a valid token.

## Tests before code
Unit: token generation and hashing (length, URL-safe, hash differs from token, constant-time compare), invitation expiry arithmetic at exactly 72 h, activation decision validation, gate and session-state rules for each status. Integration (real PostgreSQL): invitation single use under concurrency (two claims, one account), expiry at the boundary, wrong or reused token indistinguishable, re-invite revokes the old token, claim creates exactly status pending with department and claim record, library failure leaves the token usable, activation needs role, department, not self, still pending, fresh factor; decline ends sessions; suspended and left cannot reach any portal path; pending is refused on pages and endpoints but may enrol and sign out; no row stays active; log append-only; cascade. Browser: PJ1 end to end with a virtual authenticator, expired and reused links, a head of another department sees nothing, pending person sees the waiting page and nothing else, decline, 390 px, keyboard.

## Not in this slice
Email delivery of the link (no mailer; the command prints it), the 24-hour delayed recovery for "lost everything", HR marking someone as left (a command sets the status; no HR screen), quarterly access review, the breached-password check (AUTH-03), a real People import, delegation.
