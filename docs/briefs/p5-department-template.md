# Brief: department and discipline template (P5, agent 4), 2026-11-20

**Objective.** One rich page for a department (T3) and one for each discipline it teaches (T4), built as a template other departments reuse. First instance: Department of Computer and Software Technologies (DCST) and its five BS disciplines: Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence. Owner request, 2026-11-20 16:32.

**Design.** A department or discipline gets the rich layout when a *profile* exists for its slug (`apps/web/src/site/academics/profiles/`). Without a profile the existing plain pages are unchanged, so no other department breaks. A profile is typed data (about, highlights, labs, research, careers, curriculum by year, tracks, skills, FAQ, pictures). Names, level, duration, head, people and e-mail still come from the CMS records; the profile only adds what the CMS has no field for. Moving a profile into CMS fields later does not change the components.

**Sections.** Department: hero and quick facts, overview with vision and mission, why this department, disciplines (cards with pictures), labs and facilities, research areas, careers, student life, people, FAQ, contact. Discipline: hero and facts, overview, why study it, learning outcomes, curriculum by year (tabs), tracks, tools, careers, admission steps, FAQ, the other disciplines of the department.

**Labelling (owner decision 2026-11-20).** No "Sample" pill or prefix on these pages; real discipline names. The text I wrote about labs, courses and careers is illustrative, not sourced. Each profile has `status: 'draft'`, which shows one quiet line at the foot of the page; setting `reviewed` after the department approves removes it. The `sample.invalid` source on the records stays (the machine marker the seeds, the integrity test and the database use).

**Pictures.** Original SVG illustrations generated by `apps/web/scripts/make-academic-art.mjs` (brand colours, no people, no logos), committed under `public/images/academics/`; the owner's campus photo for the contact block. Served by the CDN.

**Paths touched.** New: `apps/web/src/site/academics/**`, `apps/web/scripts/make-academic-art.mjs`, `apps/web/public/images/academics/**`, tests. Changed: `site/organisation/{Pages,routes}.tsx` (hand the profile over), `scripts/import/seeds/{data,apply}.ts` (DCST and its programmes replace Sample Computer Science). No schema, token or route file is edited.

**Risks.** (1) Invented text read as fact: mitigated by the draft line and by keeping numbers derived from the lists. (2) Seed change breaks the pack integrity test. (3) Long pages at 390 px and right-to-left. (4) Profile shape wrong for a department of another kind (e.g. Law): that is the point of the second instance.

**Tests.** Unit: profile integrity (every programme slug has a profile, every picture exists, no empty lists, no duplicate ids). Browser: both page kinds at 1440, 1024, 390 px, left to right and right to left, overflow and axe. **Gate:** the owner looks at the DCST pages and says the template is right, then the other departments follow.
