# FP-17 results: claim by invitation and activation by the department head (FP-01, AUTH-01, PJ1, section 5.6)

Date 2026-11-21. Branch `fp/claim-activation`, stacked on `fp/review-queue` (PR #115): merge #115 first. Brief `docs/briefs/fp17-claim-activation.md`; predictions FP17-a and FP17-b. All people are Sample; nothing real, nothing live; no email is sent (there is no mailer), the operator command prints the link.

## What was built
- Migration 0012: `invitation` (token hash only, 72 hours, one open invitation per record, one claim per record), `account_status` (pending-activation, active, suspended, left; no row means active so the existing sample accounts are unchanged), `head_role`, and an append-only `account_review` log.
- `pnpm identity:invite <record> <email> <name> <department>` prints the claim link once; inviting the same record again revokes the earlier link. `pnpm identity:roles` gained `grant-head`, `revoke-head` and `set-status <email> <active|suspended|left>` (suspended and left end every session).
- `/portal/claim/<token>`: a public page, matched by exact shape (43 URL-safe characters), sent `no-referrer` and `noindex`. It shows only the invited name and address. A password of 15 to 128 characters creates the account through the library, then the link is used and the pending status, department and claim are written in one transaction; if the link cannot be used the new account is removed. A used, expired, revoked, made-up or malformed link all show the same page.
- Account status is read in `readSessionState`, where pages and the API both read the session, and is a required dependency (a caller that forgets it does not compile). Pending: enrolment, sign-out, reading the session, the first recovery codes and `/portal/pending` only. Suspended or left: session ended, treated as signed out. Unreadable: portal closed.
- `/portal/pending`: waiting page with only Sign out; sends the person home once active.
- `/portal/activate` for department heads: pending people from their own department who have saved a sign-in method, as cards (name, invited address, time). Confirm or "Not ours" (decline suspends the account and ends its sessions, with a confirm dialog). Needs a recent second factor. One transaction: head role locked, same department (case ignored), not oneself, still pending, a factor enrolled; log row.

## Defects found
- Real: a pending account was refused the endpoint that issues its first recovery codes, which stopped the enrolment flow. Found by the browser run; fixed; tests assert it and that all other portal endpoints stay closed.
- Real: the activation table overflowed at 390 px with long text (found by a harder check); replaced with cards.
- Test gaps found by mutation: store-level single use; activation service unit tests.
- My own test mistakes: one (tied clock).

## Not run, and why
- Email delivery of the link: there is no mailer; the command prints it. Rate limiting of the claim action: the 256-bit link is the control; an abuse limit per address is not added.
- A forged server-action call in the browser (covered by unit and integration tests); real devices, a screen reader, https; HR marking someone as left from a screen (a command does it); the 24-hour delayed recovery; quarterly access review; breached-password check; a real People import and real department heads.
- Departments and record references are Sample text typed by the operator; they will come from People when real data is used.

## Decisions I made (owner delegated decisions to me on 2026-11-21)
- The activation list is a separate page from the profile review queue: a department head is not necessarily a reviewer, and the two lists answer different questions. The spec's wording ("in the reviewer queue") is met by the same department and the same second-factor rule; say if you want them merged.
- An account with no status row is active. Only the claim flow writes rows, so existing sample accounts and the seed script are unchanged; the cost is that a future code path that creates users without a row would create an active account. The claim service removes the account if the status cannot be written, for that reason.
